Shoulder blood supply: branches of the AXILLARY ARTERY (sample slide written for testing)
Part 1 — superior thoracic artery: serratus anterior and pectoral muscles
Part 2 — thoraco-acromial trunk: acromial branch, pectoral branches; lateral thoracic artery
Part 3 — subscapular artery → circumflex scapular artery and thoracodorsal artery
Anterior and posterior circumflex humeral arteries around the surgical neck
Muscles supplied: latissimus dorsi, pectoralis major, pectoralis minor, subclavius, deltoid,
supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor, subscapularis, teres major, biceps brachii
Suprascapular artery (from the thyrocervical trunk) joins the scapular anastomosis
Cords of the brachial plexus surround the second part of the artery
